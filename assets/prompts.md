# Fundos do catálogo

Gerados com a ferramenta integrada de geração de imagens, usando a imagem enviada pelo usuário como direção visual. Os escudos e horários são inseridos depois pelo código; não aparecem nos fundos.

## Vertical — stadium-portrait.jpg

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: reusable background image for a football match card in a streaming catalog. Create a premium, high-end sports broadcast key art background: night football stadium, deep cobalt blue on the left merging into rich violet on the right, dramatic floodlights and subtle crowd texture. A polished silver football trophy is centered in the far background and deliberately subdued so foreground elements can be composited over it. Strong depth, realistic lighting, crisp professional sports design, restrained energy, no excessive particles. Composition must have generous clear space in the upper-middle left and right for two large club crests, and clear space in the lower third for a kickoff time. Absolutely no team crests, no flags, no people, no letters, no words, no numbers, no watermark. Portrait composition roughly 2:3, suitable for crop to a wide banner too.

## Horizontal — stadium-wide.jpg

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: 16:9 landscape background for a football match detail banner. Match the visual language of a premium sports broadcast: night football stadium, cinematic deep cobalt blue on the left blending into rich violet on the right, focused stadium floodlights and crowd atmosphere. A polished silver football trophy sits far back at the center, visible but subdued; foreground middle-left and middle-right must remain clear for two team crests, and lower center clear for a kickoff time. Photographic realism, art directed, crisp and restrained, no excessive particles or fake UI. Absolutely no team crests, no flags, no people, no letters, no words, no numbers, no watermark.
